2. Data We Collect

This application stores the following data to provide its services:

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Data is processed exclusively for the purpose of operating the dart club ranking system, including match management, player ranking, and administrative functions.

4. Cookies

This website uses the following cookies:

No third-party tracking or analytics cookies are used.
